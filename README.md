## Research Experience

### Robust Decoding Under Heavy-Tailed Cauchy Noise
**Undergraduate Research Assistant — American University of Beirut**

Worked under the supervision of **Prof. Jihad Fahs** and **Prof. Ibrahim Abou-Faycal** on robust decoding techniques for communication systems operating under heavy-tailed noise.

- Derived a **closed-form decoding rule for BPSK signals under Cauchy noise**, extending the GRAND/ORBGRAND framework beyond its conventional Gaussian-noise setting
- Developed a lower-complexity approximation that achieved **rank correlation τ ≥ 0.93** with the exact decoding metric
- Evaluated the approach through simulations on random linear codes
- Demonstrated up to **3.7× lower block-error rate** than standard hard-decision decoding in the evaluated setting

`Robust Decoding` `GRAND` `Signal Processing` `Statistical Modeling` `MATLAB`
